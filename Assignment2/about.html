What is World of Warcraft?

World of warcraft is an MMORPG (Massive Multiplayer Online Role Playing Game). It's a subscription based game with a deep lore and many social mechanics such as guilds and matchmaking content. The game is entirely based around the Warcraft series, orginally an RTS(Real time strategy) game and has a set of books filled with lore.
